## 1. Core Executive & Operations Dashboard
**Description:** A centralized real-time mission control center displaying overall business health, active portfolio statistics, upcoming renewal alerts, and financial breakdowns.
**Features:**
* **Real-time KPI Metric Cards:** Live visual count of Total Customers, Total Policies, Active Coverages, and Policies Expiring within 30 days.
* **Renewal Reminders Hub:** Interactive popup filtering policies due for renewal in 15, 30, 45, 60, 90, 180, or 365 days.
* **Today's Overview Multi-column Modal:** Instant snapshot of today's birthdays, wedding anniversaries, policy renewals, scheduled meetings, and pending follow-ups.
* **Direct WhatsApp Wishes:** Single-click WhatsApp greeting button for client birthdays and policy anniversary dates.
* **Category Policy Filter Tabs:** Instant portfolio filtering across Health, Motor, Life, SME, Travel, Fire, Marine, and other lines of business.
* **Interactive Policy Mix Donut Chart:** Visual breakdown of policies by insurance type, top segment highlight, and aggregate gross premium volume.
* **Policy Expiry Exposure Bar Chart:** Visual forecasting showing policies maturing over upcoming months to plan proactive retention campaigns.
* **Insurer Performance Ranking:** Leaderboard ranking insurance companies by volume of issued policies, active retention rate, and total gross premium.
* **Staff & Sub-Agent Commission Ledger:** Live table displaying recent policy commission payouts (Agent/Staff name, Policy number, Premium, Staff commission, Admin profit, and payout status).
* **Commission & Admin Profit Analytics:** Visual profit-split donut chart depicting Net Admin Profit versus Agent/Staff Payout, accompanied by quick cards for Total Gross Premium, Admin Comm., and Net Earnings.

## 2. AI Policy Upload & OCR Engine
**Description:** An intelligent document extraction pipeline that automatically reads insurance policy PDF documents and transforms them into structured CRM policies without manual data entry.
**Features:**
* **Drag-and-Drop PDF Upload:** High-speed document uploader handling documents up to 20 MB with immediate preview.
* **Multi-Page Optical Text & Image Recognition:** Intelligent extraction pipeline combining native PDF text parsing with fallback OCR for scanned policy schedules.
* **Automated Data Review Screen:** 4-step wizard (Upload &rarr; Extract &rarr; Review &rarr; Save) with side-by-side synchronized PDF viewer and editable form.
* **Intelligent Entity Matching:** Automatically matches and links extracted company names, policy types, subtypes, branches, and client profiles against database records.
* **Auto-Extraction of Key Insurance Fields:** Captures Policy Number, Premium, Sum Insured / Sum Assured, Policy Start & Expiry Dates, Customer Contact Info, Nominee Details, and Intermediary Codes.
* **Family Members & Covered Lives Detection:** Reads schedules and tabular lists to automatically populate covered family members (Name, Relationship, DOB, Gender).
* **Add-on Covers & Riders Breakdown:** Detects and parses optional riders, endorsements, and policy add-on covers along with individual rider premiums.
* **Interactive Field Suggestions:** Displays AI-extracted alternate candidate values directly beneath fields with a single click to accept.
* **Inline Quick-Add Modals:** Fast `+` button creation for missing clients, branches, subtypes, or insurance providers directly inside the review screen without losing progress.
* **AI Policy Renewal Integration:** Dedicated "Renew an existing policy with AI" workflow that links new policy documents directly to their prior term.

## 3. Policy Center (General & Motor Insurance)
**Description:** A full-featured policy management module designed to record, track, view, and renew health, motor, fire, marine, and commercial insurance policies.
**Features:**
* **Comprehensive Policy Registry:** Master list with full-text search, multi-filter dropdowns (Client, Insurer, Policy Type, Date range), and status tracking.
* **In-Depth Policy Profiles:** Detailed view showing holder info, vehicle specifications (Registration No, Engine No, Chassis No, Make/Model), premium tax breakdown, and issue dates.
* **Insured Family & Member List:** Embedded tabular view of all family members protected under floater/group policies.
* **Add-on Covers Ledger:** Itemized list of extra covers (e.g., Zero Depreciation, Roadside Assistance, Engine Protect, Consumables).
* **Policy Document Vault:** Secure upload, storage, version history, and single-click inline PDF viewer for original insurance schedules.
* **Automated Policy Renewal Processing:** Seamless one-click policy rollover carrying over insured assets and calculating new validity dates.
* **Invoice & Certificate Generation:** Auto-generation and download of branded policy invoices and confirmation receipts in PDF format.
* **Exporting & Reporting:** Export policy registers to Excel/CSV for insurer reconciliation and operational audits.

## 4. Life Insurance Management
**Description:** A dedicated life insurance module built specifically for individual life, term plans, endowment, and pension policies.
**Features:**
* **Life Policy Recording:** Specialized fields for Sum Assured, Plan Name, Term Period, Premium Paying Term (PPT), and Payment Frequency (Yearly, Half-Yearly, Quarterly, Monthly).
* **Nominee & Beneficiary Records:** Capture nominee names, relationships, age/DOB, and allocation percentages.
* **Policy Renewal & Premium Calendars:** Track recurring premium payment dates and overdue status alerts.
* **Direct Policy Linking:** Tie life insurance policies directly to registered Clients and Family Groups.
* **Document Attachment:** Secure storage for proposal forms, medical test records, and policy bonds.

## 5. Alteration & Endorsements Management
**Description:** Manages post-issuance policy alterations, modifications, endorsements, and financial corrections.
**Features:**
* **Endorsement Logging:** Record changes such as address change, vehicle transfer, nominee update, sum insured correction, and correction of member details.
* **Endorsement Types:** Differentiates between Financial Endorsements (premium impact) and Non-Financial Endorsements (clerical corrections).
* **Document Attachment:** Upload and archive endorsement copies directly linked to the parent policy.
* **Status Lifecycle Tracking:** Track requests through Draft, Pending with Insurer, Approved, and Completed stages.

## 6. Claim Tracker
**Description:** End-to-end insurance claim lifecycle tracking from initial intimation and hospital/surveyor coordination to final settlement.
**Features:**
* **Claim Intimation Filing:** Record date of intimation, claim type (Cashless / Reimbursement), claim reason, and estimated claim amount.
* **Multi-Party Coordination:** Map claims to Family Groups, specific Insured Members, Insurance Companies, and Third-Party Administrators (TPAs).
* **Hospitalization / Incident Details:** Store hospital name, admission/discharge dates, surveyor details, and FIR/loss reports.
* **Stage Tracking:** Step-by-step progress tracking: Claim Initiated &rarr; Documents Submitted &rarr; Query Raised &rarr; Approved / Settled &rarr; Rejected.
* **Settlement Accounting:** Track approved settlement amounts, deductions, and payment reference dates.
* **Document Storage:** Central upload for discharge summaries, medical bills, diagnostic reports, and surveyor assessment sheets.

## 7. Client & Customer Management
**Description:** A 360-degree customer relationship directory housing complete personal, contact, financial, and policy records for every insured client.
**Features:**
* **Detailed Customer Profiles:** Master registry capturing full name, email, phone, secondary mobile, date of birth, wedding anniversary, PAN, Aadhaar, and address.
* **Direct Communication Hub:** Integrated buttons for WhatsApp messaging, click-to-dial phone calls, and direct email composition.
* **Family Tree / Relationship Mapping:** Link individual clients into comprehensive Family Groups with defined relations (Self, Spouse, Child, Parent).
* **Client Financial & Policy 360°:** Instant overview of all active, expired, and renewed policies held by the customer across all insurance classes.
* **KYC & Document Vault:** Store and verify customer identification documents (Aadhaar, PAN Card, Driving License, Passport, GST).
* **Status & Customer Segmentation:** Categorize clients by business priority, relationship status, lead source, and assigned sub-agent/staff.
* **Excel Import & Export:** Bulk import client lists via formatted templates and export data with applied filters.

## 10. Commission Rules & Payout Distribution
**Description:** A flexible commission calculation engine that manages insurer pay-ins, agent/staff pay-outs, and calculates net agency profit.
**Features:**
* **Multi-Tier Calculation Engine:** Configure commission rules based on percentage of gross premium, net premium, or flat currency payouts.
* **Automated Pay-In & Pay-Out Splitting:** Automatically calculates Insurer Pay-In (receivable from company) and Agent/Staff Pay-Out (payable to producer).
* **Company-Wise & Product-Wise Rules:** Define custom commission slabs by Insurance Company, Policy Type, Subtype, or specific plan.
* **Admin Net Profit Calculation:** Automatic ledger reporting showing net retained agency profit for every logged policy.
* **Manual Override Controls:** Option to allow authorized managers to manually adjust commission splits on specific complex policies.
* **Commission Entries Ledger:** Comprehensive audit trail detailing every policy transaction, payment status (Pending, Paid), and calculation method.
